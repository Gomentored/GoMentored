Questions? We have answers.

Everything you need to know before booking your first session.

GoMentored connects ambitious professionals with experienced mentors who have already achieved what you are working towards. You browse mentor profiles, request a session, and once approved by our team, you meet 1:1 for personalised career guidance.

Our mentors are carefully selected professionals currently working in top roles across entrepreneurship, analytics, finance, medicine, and tech sales — in cities like New York, London, Dubai, and Lahore. Many started their journeys in Pakistan, just like you.

Sessions are priced per booking. Each mentor sets their own rate based on their experience and field. Pricing is clearly displayed on each mentor profile before you request a session — no hidden fees.

Once you submit a session request, our team reviews it and confirms the booking within 24 to 48 hours. You will then receive a confirmation with the session link and any pre-session notes from your mentor.

Sessions are conducted as 1:1 video calls, typically 45 to 60 minutes. Mentors may also offer async feedback via voice notes or written responses depending on availability and your preference.

Absolutely. Many mentees book ongoing sessions with the same mentor to maintain accountability and track progress over time. You can also explore multiple mentors across different fields.
