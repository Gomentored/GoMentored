A Founder's Story

Every career has a ceiling.
The right mentor lifts it.

GoMentored was founded by Muhammad Hassan Asif with a single mission: to ensure no professional — at any stage of their journey — has to navigate their career alone.

Whether you're just starting out, switching industries, climbing toward senior leadership, or making a move across borders, the truth is the same at every stage: the right guidance, at the right moment, changes everything.

Hassan built his finance career from the ground up after returning to Pakistan with a Master's in International Financial Management from Queen Mary University of London. On paper, the credentials were strong. In reality, the path forward was anything but clear.

Like many ambitious professionals, he learned the hard way that a degree opens doors — but doesn't tell you which one to walk through. Without a mentor to turn to, he navigated his career through trial and error, second-guessing decisions, questioning every move.

And that's where the real cost of going alone showed itself.

Not having a mentor creates uncertainty. Uncertainty breeds anxiety. Anxiety turns into self-criticism. And over time, it quietly erodes your mental health — leaving you to question your worth, your pace, and your direction, even when you're doing the right things.

"Having the right mentor is essential. Someone who maps your career out with you, guides you from their own lived experience, and reminds you that the road you're walking has been walked before."

That realisation became GoMentored.

Hassan saw, firsthand, how a well-timed conversation with the right mentor can compress years of trial and error into months of focused growth — and replace doubt with direction. He launched GoMentored to make that conversation accessible to everyone: from students mapping their first move, to mid-career professionals plotting their next leap, to senior leaders refining their final mile.

Every mentor on the platform is carefully selected — practitioners currently working in their fields across analytics, finance, medicine, and tech sales, in cities like New York, London, and Dubai.

Because no matter where you are in your career, there's always a next goal, a next role, a next chapter. And there's always someone who has already lived it — ready to help you get there faster, with more clarity, and without the weight of figuring it all out alone.

Our Vision

From your first job to your last role, GoMentored connects you with someone who has walked the path you're stepping onto. Because the right mentor doesn't just accelerate your career — they protect your peace, restore your confidence, and redefine what's possible.
